The University Diploma of Technology (DUT) in **Renewable Energies and Energy Efficiency** provided advanced technical training in renewable energy systems, electrical engineering, thermodynamics, and applied energy technologies.

The curriculum combined **theoretical foundations and practical engineering applications**, including:

- Photovoltaic system sizing and simulation  
- Wind energy analysis and system integration  
- Thermal systems, HVAC, and heat transfer  
- Electrical machines and industrial control  
- Energy efficiency assessment and auditing  

This program strengthened my technical expertise and prepared me for advanced studies in renewable energy engineering.
