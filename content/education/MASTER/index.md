The Master’s program in **Electronics and Materials for Renewable Energies** provided advanced multidisciplinary skills in clean energy engineering, energy materials, and numerical modeling.  
It combined theoretical knowledge with intensive laboratory work, simulations, and applied research.

Key competencies developed during the master’s curriculum include:

- Advanced photovoltaic materials and semiconductor physics  
- Energy materials characterization (XRD, DSC, TGA, FTIR, SEM)  
- Corrosion science and surface engineering  
- Numerical modeling using **MATLAB, Python, QBlade, COMSOL**, and **Materials Studio**  
- Power electronics, energy conversion, and electrical systems  
- Optimization of renewable energy systems (PV, wind, hybrid systems)  
- Scientific research methods, publishing, and academic writing  

The program strengthened my expertise in designing, analyzing, and optimizing renewable energy technologies — preparing me for research, engineering, and industrial applications.
