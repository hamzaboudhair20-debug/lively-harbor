The **PhD in Renewable Energy and Electrical Systems** at Ibn Zohr University – ENSA focuses on advanced research in clean energy technologies, numerical modeling, and smart grid integration.

## 🎯 Specialization

**Multi-objective optimization, advanced numerical modeling, and artificial intelligence applied to renewable energy systems**, with a focus on aerodynamic analysis of wind turbines.

## 🔬 Research Focus

- **Aerodynamic analysis** of wind turbines using advanced numerical methods
- **Multi-objective optimization** for enhanced energy performance
- **Reduction of aerodynamic loads** and mechanical stress in wind systems
- **Artificial intelligence applications** in renewable energy prediction and control
- **Smart integration** of wind systems into modern power grids
- **Advanced numerical modeling** using CFD, FEA, and machine learning techniques

## 📊 Key Research Areas

- Wind turbine aerodynamics and performance optimization
- Grid integration and smart energy solutions
- Advanced numerical simulations (QBlade, COMSOL, CFX)
- Machine learning for renewable energy applications
- Power electronics and electrical systems integration
- Sustainable energy transition and decarbonization strategies

This doctoral research combines cutting-edge numerical methods with practical engineering applications to contribute to the advancement of renewable energy technologies and smart grid systems.
