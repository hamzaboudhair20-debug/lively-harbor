The Professional Bachelor's Degree in **Renewable Energies & Sustainable Development** provided advanced training in clean energy technologies, resource assessment, and sustainable engineering.

The program offered a balanced combination of **technical, analytical, and applied engineering skills**, including:

- Photovoltaic system design (on-grid / off-grid)
- Energy efficiency assessment and optimization
- Hybrid PV–battery system simulation (PVSyst, RETScreen, PV*SOL)
- Environmental impact evaluation and sustainability strategies
- Technical-economic analysis for renewable energy projects
- Basics of wind energy, solar thermal systems, and smart energy solutions

This degree strengthened my ability to design, analyze, and optimize renewable energy systems aligned with sustainable development goals.
