## **Abstract**

This project focuses on the design and modelling of a **standalone and hybrid photovoltaic (PV) system** for the **Regional Directorate of Energy and Mines (DREM), Agadir**.  
The study includes a complete evaluation of electrical loads, solar resource analysis, and the sizing of PV modules, charge controllers, batteries, and inverters.

Two configurations were investigated:

1. **Standalone (off-grid) PV system** for supplying essential loads  
2. **Hybrid PV–grid system** for improving reliability and reducing operational costs  

The sizing process considered worst-case irradiation conditions, autonomy requirements, battery depth-of-discharge, and inverter power limits.  
The final design ensures **continuous energy supply**, improved reliability, and **significant reduction in electricity consumption** from the grid.
