## **Abstract**

This project was conducted at **ECOWATT Morocco** and presents a complete techno-economic study of a **grid-connected photovoltaic (PV) system** designed for the **Proprim industrial site** located in Aït Melloul.  
The objective was to reduce energy consumption during peak-tariff periods while maximizing the integration of renewable energy.

The study includes:

- Analysis of ONEE electricity bills and load profile
- Identification of the portion of consumption that can be compensated by solar energy
- Design and sizing of a **198 kWp PV plant** using TESLA 545 Wp modules
- Determination of the number of modules, strings, and inverters
- Electrical design on the **DC side** (string configuration, cabling, protections)
- Electrical design on the **AC side** (inverter selection, earthing, protections)
- Simulation of energy production based on local irradiation
- Environmental evaluation (CO₂ emissions avoided)
- Complete financial assessment (CAPEX, OPEX, payback period)

The results show that the PV system reduces the site’s energy consumption by **about 20%**, provides significant economic savings, and contributes to Morocco’s renewable energy strategy.

## **Key Skills & Technical Competencies**

### 🔧 **Photovoltaic Engineering**
- Sizing of an on-grid solar PV plant  
- Determination of PV power based on irradiation and energy needs  
- Module selection (TESLA 545 Wp)  
- Calculation of the number of modules, tilt angle, and orientation  

### ⚡ **Electrical Design**
- DC cabling and protection (string fuses, surge protection devices, isolators)  
- AC protection coordination and grid compliance  
- Inverter selection and MPPT configuration  
- Electrical safety and grounding  

### 🧮 **Energy Analysis**
- Interpretation of electricity consumption data  
- Simulation of expected annual energy production  
- Estimation of performance ratio and energy yield  

### 💹 **Techno-Economic Evaluation**
- CAPEX and OPEX estimation  
- Financial indicators:  
  - Payback period  
  - Net savings  
  - Economic feasibility  
- Cost-benefit analysis over the system lifetime  

### 🌍 **Environmental Impact**
- CO₂ emissions avoided over 20–25 years  
- Energy sustainability contribution
