## 📚 Overview

This academic practical work (TP – Travaux Pratiques) is part of the **4th cycle curriculum** (PhD level) in Renewable Energy and Electrical Systems at **Ibn Zohr University – ENSA**.

The work provides comprehensive practical training on **wind turbine aerodynamic analysis and design** using the open-source numerical tool **QBlade**, combining theoretical knowledge with hands-on engineering practice.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Students completing this practical work will be able to:

- Master the **QBlade computational environment** for wind turbine design and analysis
- Perform **aerodynamic analysis** using the Blade Element Momentum Theory (BEMT) and panel methods
- Generate and optimize **airfoil polars** using XFoil integration
- Analyze the influence of blade geometry on **performance coefficients** (Cp, Cm, torque)
- Conduct **structural and dynamic simulations** of wind turbine blades
- Execute **techno-economic assessments** for wind energy projects
- Use advanced optimization techniques for **sustainable energy systems**

## 📋 Practical Topics Covered

- Wind turbine design principles and aerodynamics fundamentals
- Airfoil selection, characterization, and optimization
- Blade Element Momentum Theory (BEMT) applied to wind turbines
- Advanced numerical simulations and sensitivity analysis
- Structural analysis and fatigue assessment
- Grid integration and energy yield predictions
- Real-world case studies and site-specific applications

## 📄 Technical Report

The complete technical report **"Tp-Qblade S3.pdf"** includes:

- Detailed methodology and theoretical background
- Step-by-step QBlade workflow documentation
- Practical exercises and assignment instructions
- Results analysis and interpretation guidelines
- Advanced topics in wind turbine optimization and control
